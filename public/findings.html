Regulation 1 of 5

More scrutiny of ingredient safety

More suppliers may need maintained safety dossiers and rapid replacement options; historical use alone becomes a weaker commercial assurance.

Novel ingredients face regional access gates

Technology disruptions

Technology 1 of 5

AI links formulation to sensory outcomes

Models could recommend recipes within taste, cost, legal and supply constraints, with automated experiments closing the loop.

Fermentation as an ingredient factory

High-value sweeteners, flavour molecules and selected functional proteins are plausible early winners; bulk replacement remains conditional on process economics.

Read more →Technology 3 of 5

Enzymes change ingredients inside the food

More manufacturers could tailor sweetness, texture and functionality during processing; suppliers sell a process recipe with the enzyme.

Food structure controls texture and flavour

Microgels, emulsions and encapsulation could recover creaminess with less fat, control flavour release or protect sensitive aromas.

Useful ingredients from food side streams

Improved separation and processing could recover more fibre, protein and flavour value from food-production side streams.
