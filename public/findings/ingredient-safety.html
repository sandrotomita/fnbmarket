Regulation 1 of 5

More scrutiny of ingredient safety

More suppliers may need maintained safety dossiers and rapid replacement options; historical use alone becomes a weaker commercial assurance.

Highest strategic impact · 2026–2030 pressure, continuing through 2036 · High confidence in direction

Evidence today

The US proposed mandatory GRAS notices in August 2026. EU withdrawals already affect eight smoke flavourings. Red No. 3 has a US food reformulation deadline in January 2027.

Plausible change by 2036

By 2036, more suppliers may need maintained safety dossiers and rapid replacement options. Historical use alone becomes a weaker commercial assurance.

Example and industry impact

A barbecue seasoning supplier qualifies alternative flavour systems, checks contaminant profiles and validates taste with customers. Manufacturers absorb testing and relaunch costs. Suppliers with credible replacement libraries gain business.

Status and limits

EU withdrawals and Red No. 3 action are enacted. The US GRAS change remains a proposal, not blanket pre-market approval.

Analyst notes

The EU transition for foods containing the eight affected smoke primary products ended on 1 July 2026 for most categories, with 1 July 2029 for specified cheese, meat and fish categories and relevant subcategories. Eligible existing stocks can remain until minimum durability/use-by dates. This is not a ban on every smoky taste or traditional smoking.

FDA says GRAS notification is currently voluntary and the August 2026 proposed rule would require notices for certain uses if finalised. Red No. 3 food deadline: 15 January 2027.

Forecast: broader dossier maintenance and reformulation demand; no prediction that all current additives will be prohibited.
