This matrix is an original scenario artefact inspired by Shell-style uncertainty framing. The central planning case is chosen for robustness, not assigned a probability. Regulatory alignment concerns acceptance of evidence and approvals, not whether rules are weak or strong. Across all four futures, taste, cost-in-use, reliable supply and credible evidence remain useful investment priorities.

Wildcards are not base-case predictions. Individualised microbiome foods, broad cultivated-meat displacement and autonomous AI food approval are excluded from the central case because evidence, economics or regulation remain too uncertain. A universal ultra-processed-food tax is not assumed.
